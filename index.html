ORBIT.

CHAPTER 02 · P. 11

One timestep.
Then another.

An orbit is never solved, only approximated: the integrator takes a small step, commits a small error, and promises to keep it below tolerance.1 The trajectory above is being integrated by your scrolling. You are, at this moment, the timestep. Choose it wisely.
